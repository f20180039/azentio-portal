This is a prototype placeholder, not Azentio's privacy policy. Azentio's own policy is on azentio.com.

Everything in this prototype is synthetic: the organisations, people, observations and figures are fictional. Please do not enter real personal or customer data.

What the prototype stores

An email address and a hashed session token when you sign in, what you type into forms, and an audit log of changes. Sign-in codes and invitations for the demo personas are shown on screen; other email is queued, not sent, unless an email provider is configured.

What it shares

Client observations stay private to their organisation unless the author proposes a de-identified copy and a moderator publishes it. Shared copies carry no names or organisation, month-level dates, and sector and region only when enough organisations share them.

AI providers

AIRA sends only moderator-approved shared copies, or one synthetic sandbox run, to the AI provider to answer a question. Questions and answers are kept in your browser tab, not stored by the portal.
