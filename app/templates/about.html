About azentio.ai

azentio.ai builds enterprise AI applications for banks, lenders and insurers: decisioning, document intelligence, reporting and analytics, designed for human oversight and audit.

How collective learning works here

Clients record market observations privately. They may propose a de-identified version for the learning community. Moderators review and publish only the approved copy. Theme statistics appear only when at least three different organisations contribute, and product owners link improvement ideas to the evidence, with a plain status.

This is a collective learning hub, not federated machine learning: no model is trained on contributions, and AIRA summaries do not retrain anything.
