What this build runs on

  • Python and FastAPI, server-rendered pages; no access decision is made in the browser.
  • SQLite with full-text search for shared copies; withdrawal removes an item from search in the same transaction.
  • Open-weight models on Groq for AIRA, Google Gemini as backup, and a non-AI fallback; no private record is ever sent to the model.
  • Synthetic data only. Production would add single sign-on, managed email and a networked database.
