This is a prototype placeholder, not a formal accessibility statement. The prototype uses synthetic data and has not been audited against WCAG.

What we aimed for

  • Every control works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline, including the dropdowns, the site menu and the AIRA panel.
  • Pages use headings, labelled form fields and landmarks; new AIRA answers are announced to screen readers.
  • Light and dark themes, reduced motion respected, and layouts that fit a phone screen without sideways scrolling.

Known gaps

No formal audit or assistive-technology testing has been done. Some data tables scroll sideways on small screens.
